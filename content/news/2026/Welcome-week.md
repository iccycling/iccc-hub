Welcome (back) to Imperial! Whether you're a Fresher, a returning member or just curious about cycling in London, we'd love to have you involved.

**Good news: no membership is needed this week.** Just show up and ride with us.

## 👋 Welcome Fair – Tuesday 29 Sep

Come and say hello at **Stand 27**, from **10am to 4pm**. Whether you have questions about rides, kit or joining the club, or just want to chat about bikes, we'd be happy to see you.

Also worth a visit: the **Triathlon Club at Stand 59**. Ask them about the cycling options they offer too.

## 🚴 Richmond Park Laps – Wednesday 30 Sep

Meet at the **Roehampton Gate Café** ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Roehampton+Gate+Cafe+Richmond+Park)).

- **2:00pm – Beginner Laps:** a relaxed pace, and everybody is welcome.
- **2:30pm – Main Laps:** for those who want to pick up the pace a bit.

## 🌲 MTB Ride – Saturday 3 Oct, Swinley Forest

All levels are welcome! Further details are shared in our WhatsApp group. If you're not in it yet, send us a DM on Instagram or get in touch another way (see below).

## 🛣️ Longer Group Ride – Sunday 4 Oct

- **Start:** 8:00am at the Roehampton Gate Café
- **Pace:** 28–30 km/h group riding
- **Distance:** 50+ km
- The route will be shared on WhatsApp.

## 📬 Get in touch

All current students and alumni are welcome. Message us on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/iccycling) or by email, or just come and find us at the Fair. We're looking forward to riding with you!
