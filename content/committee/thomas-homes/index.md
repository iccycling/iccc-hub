## About Me

I got into cycling through watching the Tour de France and riding with my dad, and I've been firmly into road cycling ever since.

One of my favourite things about the club is the chat on rides — it's a big part of what makes the club such a great place to be. As President this year, I want to help make sure everyone feels welcome and that the club remains a friendly and approachable place, whether you're completely new to cycling or have been riding for years.

I'm particularly looking forward to the BUCS TTs this year, and hopefully plenty of good rides, good chats and good coffee stops along the way.
